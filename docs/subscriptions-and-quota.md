# Subscriptions and Quota

Open "Subscriptions and Quota" in the navigation bar: when adding a subscription, record the name, agent, amount, currency, billing period, validity dates, and weekly cycle. The records are stored in the local Observatory database, and the end date does not include that day; subscriptions that are disabled or that fall outside their validity dates can be edited or deleted under "Subscription Management → All Subscriptions".

The billing period and the quota cycle are two different things: the billing period says how often you pay (monthly, yearly, or a custom "every N days"), while the weekly cycle says when the quota resets. A custom billing period requires a number of days between 1–366.

The weekly cycle is computed with the local time zone and the configured weekday, hours, minutes, and seconds; it includes the start and excludes the end. The cycle rolls forward automatically over time; use the previous cycle, the historical cycle dropdown, or the next cycle to view the other cycles. The current cycle only counts the records up to the moment of the query, and no query is run for future cycles.

Cycle navigation is limited to the **subscription validity period**: a cycle that does not intersect the validity period covers no subscription quota, does not appear in the historical dropdown, and the previous-cycle and next-cycle buttons are greyed out when a boundary is reached.

The cycle itself is **clamped** to the subscription validity period: the start takes the later of "subscription start time" and "weekly cycle start time", and the end takes the earlier of "weekly cycle end time" and "subscription expiry moment". When a subscription starts or expires in the middle of a cycle, only the portion covered by the subscription is counted, and the page marks the ranges before and after clamping.

Choosing "Recent days" allows querying today, the last 7, 30, or 90 days, or a custom 1–366 days, including today. The trend chart can switch between tokens, cost, and requests, with a left axis and hover tooltips, and it shows at most the most recent 30 dates; the summary at the top covers the entire selected range. The query only reads the already-collected data, and the most recent scan time is shown at the bottom of the page.

The cost and the tokens are the **all local sources** statistics of the selected agent, and are not limited by the LAN sharing selection. The weekly cycle is clamped to the subscription validity period, while a recent-days query is not clamped; when falling back to full date ranges, usage from outside the original cycle may be included, and the page warns about it. Several subscriptions of the same agent cannot currently be distinguished by account; the subscription amounts are shown separately and are not added to the estimated API cost. The subscription amounts show the symbol of the selected currency (USD `$`, CNY `¥`, EUR `€`), but no exchange-rate conversion is performed — the API cost is always measured in USD. The subscription dates and amounts are maintained manually and do not represent the provider's invoice; the authoritative remaining quota and percentage are the ones shown on the provider's own page.

If a historical record only has a daily rollup and a cycle boundary cuts through that date, the page shows a precision error instead of silently counting that part of the usage as zero. The error lists the **affected dates** and offers "switch to a full date range query" to count those days in full; you can also go back to the cycle query or switch to a cycle that still has details. When daylight saving time makes the configured local time non-existent, the page prompts you to adjust the cycle.

"Subscription Management" supports searching by name or agent and sorting by start date, name, or amount; each row can be enabled or disabled directly, and you can jump to that subscription's usage page to review the historical usage. Subscriptions that are not yet effective or that have been disabled can be viewed the same way. The position preferences of the page (current subscription, tab, filters, statistic mode, day count, trend metric) are stored locally and restored after a reload.
